The Japanese Steamless TH20 v1.00a target is pinned to SHA-256
`a274b45fe6ec53511718bb328c2ff169a74e67f95d1b0c74d97d348b955a0897`.
Its entire Card Draw Boss `0x00487590..0x004878AC` has original SHA-256
`d2db56d68307f6ab895a94c9b44cc8e73c88a2b11de030d2240882efb453f7e6`,
**797B /221 x86 instructions /30 E8 calls /28 PE HIGHLOW**. Both the

## Reconstructed gameplay

The new [`src/CardDrawBoss797.cpp`](../src/CardDrawBoss797.cpp) reads
the actual `Card` flag word at +`0x78`, resolves the third info-animation
handle and retrieves its packed RGBA alpha through the independently exact
19B Animation color getter. It prepares the existing process-owned AsciiInf
renderer, then displays either the real `bonus` value or the literal `$`,
with the original Vector3 positions (X **266**, **282** and **360**, Y
**37**). It selects capture/attempt records from the existing Card record
archive according to `Session::field_70_is_two()`, preserving each
independent native archive and record lookup. The HUD displays **MASTER**
after 100 captures, **`%.2d/99+`** for capped attempts or **`%.2d/%.2d`**
for the two counters; the renderer's original font, layer and alpha
settings are reset after drawing. The physical original addresses of all
four literal strings and the four float constants are pinned in the
original-only verifier.

All real calls are typed and belong to existing AnimationHandle,
Animation, Card, Session and AsciiInf owners. The original native
**variadic text formatter** is a `__cdecl` *member* on a borrowed AsciiInf
view, so MSVC pushes the renderer receiver last, after the format and
numeric parameters. A first `std::string_view`-explicit probe emitted
**919B /254 instructions**. Replacing the literal text arguments with
ordinary narrow strings brought it to **824B /229**; recovering the
color byte as `AnimationColorChannels::alpha` without an invented
address-calculation temporary produced **810B /225**. The correct
variadic member declaration finally produced the entire original
**797B /221**, **30/30 original physical E8 sites** and **28/28 PE
HIGHLOW sites**, with no artificial local, NOP or assembly.

The three separate authored AsciiInf setters, each **25/25B whole exact**
with 10 instructions and no relocations, are reconstructed together in
[`src/CardDrawAsciiSetters75.cpp`](../src/CardDrawAsciiSetters75.cpp):

| Native head | Source method | Original field |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `0x00488A00` | `set_layer_488a00` | AsciiInf `layer` at +`0x1A1EC` |

The borrowed [`NativeCardAsciiDrawView.hpp`](../src/NativeCardAsciiDrawView.hpp)
adds no AsciiInf storage; the retained `AsciiInf` offsets are statically
checked. The original Card Draw Boss calls every setter twice.
